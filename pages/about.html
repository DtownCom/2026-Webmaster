About Us

Learn about the people and mission behind the Downingtown Community Resource Hub.

Our Mission

The Downingtown Community Resource Hub is a student-built, community-focused website designed to connect residents of the Downingtown area with the local support and services they need. Whether you're looking for food assistance, housing help, healthcare, education, transportation, or ways to give back, we aim to make those resources easy to find in one place.

Who We Are

This site was created by students participating in the STEM Webmaster challenge. Our team researched, designed, and built this hub to serve the real needs of our community. We believe everyone deserves access to the resources that help them thrive — and technology can help make that happen.
