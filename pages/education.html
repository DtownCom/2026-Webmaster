What schools does Downingtown offer?

Downingtown Elementary Schools

The Downingtown Area School District (DASD) operates multiple elementary schools serving the Downingtown region and its surrounding townships. These schools provide core academic instruction in literacy, math, science, social studies, and the arts, along with social-emotional support and community engagement activities designed to prepare students in kindergarten through grade 5 (or grade 6 at specialized campuses) for future academic success.

Downingtown Middle Schools

DASD’s middle schools offer education tailored to early adolescents, focusing on academic rigor, critical thinking, and personal growth across subjects like language arts, math, science, and social studies. Programs at schools such as Lionville Middle and Downingtown Middle include exploratory classes and support services that help students navigate the transition from childhood to young adulthood within a structured learning environment, making kids high-school-ready.

Visit Resource

Downingtown High Schools

Downingtown Area School District’s high schools—including Downingtown East, Downingtown West, and specialized campuses like the Downingtown STEM Academy—provide students with opportunities for advanced coursework, career readiness, extracurricular engagement, and post-secondary planning. These schools support a wide range of academic tracks, from traditional college preparatory classes to specialized programs in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics. They are all very highly ranked throughout the state.

Downingtown Community Education Foundation

The Downingtown Community Education Foundation is a volunteer-led nonprofit that partners with DASD to inspire students and enrich educational experiences. Through grants, scholarships, special projects, and community events, the Foundation provides teachers and students with additional resources that expand learning opportunities beyond the classroom, such as STEAM programs, technology initiatives, academic support, and family engagement activities.
