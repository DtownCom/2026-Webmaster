Education Resources

Learning opportunities for every age — schools, libraries, tutoring, and early intervention

Downingtown Elementary Schools

The Downingtown Area School District (DASD) operates multiple elementary schools serving the Downingtown region and its surrounding townships. These schools provide core academic instruction in literacy, math, science, social studies, and the arts, along with social-emotional support and community engagement activities designed to prepare students in kindergarten through grade 5 (or grade 6 at specialized campuses) for future academic success.

Visit Resource

Downingtown Middle Schools

DASD’s middle schools offer education tailored to early adolescents, focusing on academic rigor, critical thinking, and personal growth across subjects like language arts, math, science, and social studies. Programs at schools such as Lionville Middle and Downingtown Middle include exploratory classes and support services that help students navigate the transition from childhood to young adulthood within a structured learning environment, making kids high-school-ready.

Visit Resource

Downingtown High Schools

Downingtown Area School District’s high schools—including Downingtown East, Downingtown West, and specialized campuses like the Downingtown STEM Academy—provide students with opportunities for advanced coursework, career readiness, extracurricular engagement, and post-secondary planning. These schools support a wide range of academic tracks, from traditional college preparatory classes to specialized programs in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics. They are all very highly ranked throughout the state.

Visit Resource

Downingtown Community Education Foundation

The Downingtown Community Education Foundation is a volunteer-led nonprofit that partners with DASD to inspire students and enrich educational experiences. Through grants, scholarships, special projects, and community events, the Foundation provides teachers and students with additional resources that expand learning opportunities beyond the classroom, such as STEAM programs, technology initiatives, academic support, and family engagement activities.

Visit Resource

Delaware County Community College – Downingtown Center

The DCCC Downingtown Center is a 46,000-square-foot campus located at 100 Bond Drive, built on the site of the historic Downingtown Industrial and Agricultural School. It features a 16,000-square-foot STEM wing, a nursing simulation lab, computer classrooms, a learning resource center, and student lounge areas. Students can complete full degree programs in Business Administration, Liberal Arts, Psychology, Computer Science, Education, Engineering, Health Studies, and Nursing (RN) without leaving Chester County. Phone: 484-237-6200.

Downingtown Library

The Downingtown Library proudly serves over 53,000 residents in the greater Downingtown area. Located at 122 Wallace Ave, the library offers free book borrowing, digital resources including Libby/OverDrive e-books, Mango Languages, and Kanopy streaming, as well as museum pass reservations and a rentable study room. Community programs include story times for toddlers and infants, book clubs, a Conversational English Group, gentle yoga, and educational workshops. Open Monday–Thursday 9am–8pm, Friday 9am–5pm, and Saturday 9am–4pm. Call (610) 269-2741.

Club Z! Tutoring of Downingtown

Club Z! Tutoring provides personalized in-home and online tutoring for students of all ages in the Downingtown area. Subjects covered include math, reading, writing, science, foreign languages, test prep (SAT/ACT), study skills, Pre-K readiness, and college-level courses. Tutors are certified, background-checked professionals matched to each student through Club Z!'s proprietary "Z! Tutor Match" system — pairing students based on academic need, personality, and learning style. A tutor-match guarantee ensures every student finds the right fit. Call (866) 442-2582 to get started.

Birth to Three Services – Chester County Intermediate Unit

The CCIU's Birth to Three program is Chester County's Infant/Toddler Early Intervention service for children from birth through age three who have developmental delays or disabilities. Services — including speech and language therapy, occupational therapy, physical therapy, hearing support, and specialized instruction — are delivered in the child's natural environment (home or community) and are provided at no cost to families. To begin the referral process, contact Chester County Early Intervention at (610) 344-5948 or email BirthtoThree@cciu.org. The CCIU is located at 455 Boot Road, Downingtown, PA 19335.

Kumon – Downingtown

Kumon is a self-paced, individualized math and reading enrichment program serving students from preschool through high school in Downingtown. Using Kumon's proven incremental method, students build a strong academic foundation by completing short daily worksheets that advance in difficulty at each child's own pace — developing critical problem-solving skills, confidence, and study habits along the way. Kumon Downingtown offers free placement assessments to identify exactly where a child should start and tailors a custom learning plan to close gaps and accelerate progress. Contact the Downingtown center to schedule your child's free assessment today.
