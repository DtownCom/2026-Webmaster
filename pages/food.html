Food Security

Find your food related resources here!

Lord’s Pantry of Downingtown

Lord’s Pantry of Downingtown is a community pantry that provides supplemental groceries and essential food items to individuals and families experiencing food insecurity in Downingtown and nearby communities. The organization also coordinates special seasonal programs and depends on volunteers and donors to support its mission of reducing hunger locally.

Visit Resource

Safe Harbor of Chester County

Safe Harbor of Chester County offers food and meals as part of its broader support services for unhoused and low-income adults. In addition to providing hot meals, the organization connects residents with shelter, case management, transportation assistance, employment readiness support, and pathways to housing stability.

Visit Resource

Downingtown Area School District Food Pantry

The DASD Food Pantry serves students and families experiencing food insecurity within the Downingtown school community. By offering perishable and non-perishable food items and personal care products by appointment, the pantry aims to reduce barriers to learning and support families in maintaining stable nutrition and health throughout the school year.

Visit Resource

Chester County Food Bank

The Chester County Food Bank operates a Food Finder directory and network that helps residents locate local food pantries, meal programs, and food assistance sites across the county. This resource connects individuals and families with emergency food distribution points, mobile markets, and supplemental nutrition programs to ensure broader access to food resources throughout Chester County.

Visit Resource

Ebenezer Full Revival Baptist Church Food Pantry

The Ebenezer Full Revival Baptist Church Food Pantry is a community-based food assistance program providing free groceries and hygiene necessities. The pantry typically operates on the 2nd and 4th Saturdays of each month from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM

Visit Resource

Philabundance

Philabundance is a regional food bank founded in 1984 with the mission to drive hunger from communities today and end it for good. Working with over 350 community partners across nine counties in Southeastern Pennsylvania and Southern New Jersey — including Chester County — Philabundance distributes millions of pounds of food annually. Their online Food Finder tool helps residents quickly locate nearby pantries, meal sites, and food assistance programs supported by the network.
