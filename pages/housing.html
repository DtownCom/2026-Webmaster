Housing Partnership of Chester County

The Housing Partnership of Chester County is a local nonprofit that works to expand access to affordable housing and promote long-term housing stability. The organization provides programs related to home ownership assistance, rehabilitation and home modification, financial literacy education, and support for homeowners and renters facing challenges. Its services help families secure, maintain, or improve their housing situation through counseling, applications for assistance programs, and other resources.

Atkinson Men’s Shelter

The W.C. Atkinson Men’s Shelter in Coatesville offers safe shelter and community resources for low-income and unhoused men in the region. Beyond basic lodging, the shelter supports neighborhood revitalization and provides referrals to additional support services. Its mission includes helping residents stabilize their lives and access the broader network of housing, health, and social support available in the area, often working closely with Chester County’s 2-1-1 coordinated entry system.

Pennsylvania 2-1-1 Housing Assistance

Pennsylvania’s 2-1-1 system is a free, confidential information and referral service that connects callers with housing assistance and shelter resources statewide. Specialists help individuals and families identify emergency shelters, rental or utility assistance, eviction prevention programs, and other supportive services available through local agencies. Chester County participates in this network, so dialing 2-1-1 or using their online portal connects you quickly with tailored help.

Family Promise of Southern Chester County

Family Promise of Southern Chester County focuses on helping families with children who are experiencing or at risk of homelessness. The organization offers case management, temporary housing through programs like Promise House, and stabilization services that support families’ transitions to permanent housing. Through personalized support, life skills coaching, and connections to employment and community resources, Family Promise helps families regain independence and housing security.

Visit Resource

Chester County Shelter Hotline

The Chester County Shelter Hotline is a 24/7 emergency line connecting individuals and families experiencing homelessness with available shelter beds and immediate housing assistance across Chester County. Operated in coordination with the county's Department of Community Development and Housing, the hotline serves as the entry point into Chester County's coordinated intake system. Callers are assessed for eligibility and matched with emergency shelter, transitional housing, or rapid rehousing options. Call 610-436-6630 or simply dial 2-1-1 for immediate support.

Friends Association for Care & Protection of Children

Friends Association builds family well-being by providing opportunities and meeting the needs of children and their parents together. Located at 825 Paoli Pike, West Chester, PA, Friends Association operates the only low-barrier family shelter in Chester County, providing housing for entire families. Their programs include an Eviction Prevention Case Resolution Program, emergency family shelter, and Housing Stability Case Management that offers continued support and guidance to empower families and reduce the risk of returning to homelessness. Last year, they provided 820 adults and children with shelter or housing stabilization, prevented 283 families from experiencing homelessness, and achieved a 90% rate of families moving to or remaining in safe and stable housing. Call (610) 431-3598.

Home of the Sparrow

Home of the Sparrow partners with women facing homelessness to secure housing and achieve long-term stability. Located at 969 Swedesford Rd, Exton, PA 19341, the organization runs six programs that helped 904 women and children last year: Eviction Prevention, Shared Housing, Supportive Housing, a Women's Re-Entry Assessment Program, Pre-Senior Bridge Housing, and Graduate Outreach. Services focus on housing placement, financial literacy, education, and trauma-informed long-term support. If you are currently experiencing homelessness, call 211 or text your zip code to 898-211. For more information contact (610) 647-4940 or info@homeofthesparrow.org.
