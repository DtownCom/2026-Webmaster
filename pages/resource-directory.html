Downingtown High Schools

Downingtown Community Education Foundation

The Downingtown Community Education Foundation enhances student learning by funding innovative programs, grants, and enrichment opportunities across the district.

Educational and Behavioral Health Services

Educational and Behavioral Health Services provide counseling, mental health support, and behavioral interventions to help students succeed academically and emotionally.

Downingtown Library

Meals on Wheels of Chester County

Category: Volunteering

Meals on Wheels of Chester County relies on volunteers to deliver nutritious meals and provide vital social connection to homebound seniors across the county.

Chester County Community Foundation

Category: Volunteering

The Chester County Community Foundation connects residents with volunteer opportunities at nonprofits addressing food security, education, youth development, and community health.

Chesco Volunteer Opportunities

Category: Volunteering

Volunteer Chesco serves as the county’s central hub for matching residents with meaningful service roles across health, human services, transportation, and community support programs.

SEPTA Regional Rail

SEPTA Regional Rail provides frequent, electrified commuter-rail service connecting Chester County and surrounding suburbs to Center City Philadelphia and major regional employment hubs.

Chester County ROVER

Chesco Connect offers flexible, door-to-door shared-ride transportation across Chester County, helping residents access essential destinations where fixed-route transit is limited.

The Struble Trail

Category: Transportation

The Struble Trail is a scenic, paved multi-use path that supports walking, biking, and short-distance commuting while connecting neighborhoods, parks, and natural areas near Downingtown.

The Amtrak Keystone Service delivers fast, reliable intercity rail travel linking Chester County with Philadelphia, Harrisburg, and New York City through multiple daily departures.
