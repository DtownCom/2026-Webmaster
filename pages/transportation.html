SEPTA Regional Rail

SEPTA Regional Rail is the commuter-rail network operated by the Southeastern Pennsylvania Transportation Authority that connects downtown Philadelphia with its suburbs across five counties, including Chester County. It comprises 13 lines and more than 150 stations, providing frequent service for commuters, students, and travelers heading into Center City and beyond. Trains typically run from early morning through late evening and operate on an electrified network shared with Amtrak and freight railroads. Service helps reduce local traffic congestion and ties suburban communities more directly into the region’s economic centers.

Chester County ROVER

Chester County ROVER, now transitioned into the county-run Chesco Connect community transit program, offers flexible, door-to-door shared-ride transportation for residents throughout Chester County. Riders can schedule trips in advance for a range of needs, including medical appointments, grocery shopping, work commutes, pharmacy stops, senior center visits, and other destinations, with reduced fares available for eligible riders. This service fills gaps where fixed-route transit may be limited and provides an accessible alternative to personal vehicle use.

The Struble Trail

The Struble Trail is a paved, multi-use rail trail located in Chester County, following the scenic East Branch of the Brandywine Creek near Downingtown. Originally built along an old rail corridor, the trail currently offers several miles of gently graded path suitable for bicycling, walking, running, and casual commuting between nearby neighborhoods and parks. The route’s flat terrain, natural setting, and connections to local parks make it both a recreational asset and a non-motorized transportation corridor for residents seeking to travel short distances without a car.

Amtrak Keystone Service

The Amtrak Keystone Service is a regional passenger train operating between Harrisburg and Philadelphia, with many trains continuing to New York City. Running along the Philadelphia–Harrisburg Main Line (also known as the Keystone Corridor), this service offers higher-speed intercity rail travel with multiple daily round trips, comfortable seating, and competitive travel times for commuters and longer-distance travelers alike. Stations in Chester County, such as Paoli, Downingtown, Exton, and Coatesville, provide local access to this broader Northeast rail network.
