Volunteering & Nonprofits

Want to help out the community? Here are some resources!

Downingtown Library

Downingtown Library invites community members of various ages to support its mission by offering volunteer roles that help keep the library vibrant and accessible. Teens can participate in structured volunteer programs (for instance, helping with events and seasonal activities), and adults are encouraged to join the Friends of the Downingtown Library, a volunteer group that promotes library programs and organizes fundraising efforts like book sales. Volunteers may help with shelving, special events, or library support tasks; opportunities open seasonally and are coordinated through the library’s volunteer page.

Meals on Wheels of Chester County

Meals on Wheels of Chester County is a local nonprofit dedicated to delivering nutritious meals to homebound older adults and others unable to prepare food for themselves. Volunteers use their own vehicles to deliver hot, balanced lunches on a schedule that fits their availability, typically Monday through Friday, and provide friendly social contact that helps reduce isolation. Participation requires compassion and a commitment to serving neighbors in need; routes generally take around an hour to complete.

Visit Resource

Chester County Community Foundation

The Chester County Community Foundation connects donors and volunteers with nonprofits throughout the county. Its Volunteer Opportunities hub lists ways you can assist partner organizations working on issues like food security, youth services, education, and community health. Whether helping out at events, supporting fundraising efforts, or offering skills-based volunteerism, the Foundation acts as a bridge between civic energy and nonprofit needs.

Visit Resource

YMCA of Greater Brandywine

The YMCA of Greater Brandywine serves Chester County communities through youth development, healthy living, and social responsibility programs. Volunteers can support before- and after-school programs, summer camps, fitness classes, community events, and more at branches throughout the region, including Downingtown. Whether you have an hour a week or a full day, the Y welcomes volunteers of all ages and backgrounds to help strengthen the community.

Visit Resource

Chesco Volunteer Opportunities

Chester County’s official volunteer portal (often called Volunteer Chesco) offers residents a way to find a wide range of service roles tied to county programs and initiatives. Volunteers may assist in programs that support older adults, provide transportation, help with administrative tasks, or simply offer companionship or practical help to neighbors. The county emphasizes matching volunteers’ interests and skills with meaningful service opportunities in health, human services, and community development.
